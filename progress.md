### Character-animation contract corrected, and western bearings are now mirrored

- Fixed the contradiction that mattered most: `guest-sprite-production-brief-v0.1.md` still specified `4 columns (walk phases)` and "all 32 cells" in the block that is actually pasted into an image model, while every other document required the eight-phase cycle. - Adopted runtime mirroring. Only `S`, `SE`, `E`, `NE` and `N` are drawn; `SW`, `W` and `NW` are those rows flipped with `flipX`. Mirroring is permitted where rotation is not, because it preserves the pixel grid exactly and the foot anchor sits on the flip axis at x = 16 of a 32 px cell. `guest-walk-8dir` drops from 64 cells to 40, the six eight-bearing sheets from 288 to 180, and the whole checklist from 1208 to 1100 drawn frames.
- Wrote the four conditions mirroring depends on into `artContract.ts` as `MIRRORING_RULES`, because each fails silently: the pivot must sit on the flip axis; only the image flips, never the frame order; characters take ambient/top shading so a mirrored bearing is not lit from the wrong side; and a tool or carried-item anchor is stored as an offset from the pivot so its sign flips with the sprite. A validator now rejects any eight-bearing sheet whose pivot is off the axis.
- Removed the `64 x 80` authoring cell. Characters are authored native at `32 x 32`. Downscaling authored pixel art is the same breach as the renderer's current `0.16x` sources, and it destroys the very thing the walk contract asks for: one source pixel at `64 x 80` is 0.4 of a pixel at `32 x 32`, so the cycle's weight transfer would not survive the export.
- Replaced the list of pivot exceptions with one principle: contact with a surface uses the foot pivot, no contact uses the waterline. `guest-cold-plunge-enter-exit` and `guest-warm-spa-enter-exit` therefore move from the waterline to the foot pivot - the guest stands on the basin floor at both ends - while `guest-water-idle` and `guest-spa-seated-idle` keep the waterline.
