# Sauna Empire Decision Reconciliation - 2026-09-15

## Purpose

This file reconciles the inherited `docs/decision-log.md` with explicit product decisions approved during the Xcode preparation pass on 2026-09-15.

It does **not** replace the historical decision log. It tells agents how to interpret it safely.

## Status labels

- **STILL LOCKED** - the inherited decision still applies as written.
- **CLARIFIED** - the inherited direction still applies, but newer approved wording narrows or explains it.
- **SUPERSEDED** - do not implement the old wording; use the newer decision instead.
- **DEFERRED** - concept remains possible, but no implementation decision exists yet.

## Canonical precedence

When product documents disagree on this branch, use this order:

1. `docs/decision-reconciliation-2026-09-15.md`
2. `PRODUCT_DECISIONS_2026-09-15.md`
3. `docs/decision-log.md`
4. current dedicated system contracts under `docs/`
5. native-preparation documents such as `GAME_DESIGN.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `SYSTEMS.md`, `DATA_MODEL.md`, `BALANCE.md`, `SAVE_SYSTEM.md`, `ASSET_SPEC.md`, `CONTENT_STUDIO.md`, `XCODE_MIGRATION.md`
6. historical audits, proposals and old prototypes

A newer explicit user-approved decision overrides an older working assumption. Do not silently blend conflicting rules.

## Reconciled decisions

### 1. Game time

**Old wording:** one in-game week per real 24-hour day was described as a working balance target to validate.

**Status: SUPERSEDED / NOW LOCKED**

Canonical decision:

- one in-game week equals exactly 24 real hours for production gameplay;
- one in-game year is approximately 52 real days;
- time is persistent and canonical whether the app is open or closed;
- future competitive periods can rely on the shared fixed time base;
- debug/development acceleration may exist but must not affect production ranking/scoring.

The old `Run Week` mechanic remains a prototype/debug shortcut only.

### 2. World map, city names and generated place names

**Status: CLARIFIED**

The inherited decision remains locked that expansion comes from generated site offers rather than a world map where the player manually chooses arbitrary cities.

However:

- specific city names, venue names, neighbourhood names or market names generated by an agent are **not** canonical merely because they appear in a proposal, prototype or data file;
- only explicitly approved names are product content;
- generated names/content must carry provenance/status in the Content Studio;
- location families such as Canal, Harbour, Forest Lake etc. remain inherited design categories where already locked, but they are not automatically real city locations.

### 3. Canal / Canal Workshop naming

**Status: CLARIFIED**

The inherited decision remains authoritative:

- Canal is a location-layer/reference family;
- `Canal Workshop` is a prototype/reference composition, not a permanently locked player-facing venue name;
- the player names owned venues;
- native migration may use the Canal reference as the first behavioral/visual fixture without turning that prototype name into canonical production content.

Any native preparation document that says `Canal Workshop` should be read as `the Canal reference venue/slice` unless it is explicitly discussing the old prototype.

### 4. Two playable browser implementations

**Status: CLARIFIED - TECHNICAL REFERENCE ONLY**

The existence of both modular `src/` and `empire-rebuild/` is not a product requirement to maintain two games.

Canonical migration interpretation:

- `src/sim`, `src/save`, `src/content` are the primary executable references for implemented deterministic behavior and contracts;
- `empire-rebuild/` is a broader product/UI/flow reference only;
- native Xcode work should converge toward one production implementation;
- do not delete either legacy implementation until the relevant behavior has been captured in tests/specs/native equivalents.

### 5. Staff scheduling

**Old direction:** staff work automatically from venue programs/opening hours/facilities and the player does not micromanage shifts.

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

Canonical decision:

- the player hires and fires staff;
- the player sets venue opening hours;
- staff are automatically allocated across the required operating hours;
- the player does not create detailed shift rotas;
- ordinary venue staff are paid hourly for assigned working hours;
- an Aufguss Master cannot perform overlapping sessions;
- if a Master is assigned continuously between two sessions, that in-between time is paid work too;
- exact minimum staffing requirements by venue size/facility mix are balance/system work, not a player scheduling interface.

### 6. 24/7 opening

**Status: CLARIFIED**

The player may choose long opening hours, but early-game 24/7 operation must not become an obvious profit exploit.

Do not solve this with an arbitrary time-lock. Solve it through:

- daypart demand;
- hourly wages/minimum coverage;
- heat/energy/cleaning/operating costs;
- physical capacity and market fit;
- venue scale/brand/destination demand later in the game.

Balance tests must explicitly test 24/7 strategies and flag unrealistic early-game profitability during near-empty hours.

### 7. Staff sickness, holidays, voluntary quitting and HR drama

**Status: SUPERSEDED / OUT OF CURRENT SCOPE**

Current decision:

- do not implement sickness, holidays, resignations or HR-event simulation now;
- staff leave only because the player dismisses them or because an already-approved unpaid/insolvency rule requires it;
- revisit only if later playtesting reveals a concrete need.

### 8. Financial distress and bankruptcy

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

The inherited objective-solvency direction remains correct.

Canonical interpretation:

- negative cash creates a factual financial-decision state;
- the player may use realistic available borrowing and/or venue sale options;
- there is no arbitrary fixed one-week countdown;
- automatic bankruptcy occurs only at a financial settlement where due obligations cannot be paid and no realistic approved loan or sale path remains;
- voluntary bankruptcy remains possible;
- exact interest/balance values remain balance data.

This supersedes any interpretation of `grace period` as a simple fixed number of weeks.

### 9. Reviews and Aufguss quality feedback

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

Use multiple feedback layers because they answer different questions:

1. **First-run program review** for a new/materially revised Aufguss composition:
   - Composition
   - Execution
   - Venue Fit
   - overall result
   - one useful observation/warning
2. **Ordinary guest comments/reviews** based only on what that guest actually experienced.
3. **Steam Guide/editorial observations** as a more informed craft/value/venue-fit voice.

Aufguss quality can therefore be learned from both program diagnostics and public guest/editorial feedback. Do not collapse all feedback into one star number.

### 10. Guest memory and regulars

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

- most guest demand may remain population/market simulation;
- each venue maintains a bounded pool of persistent identifiable regulars;
- persistent regulars may retain visits, limited factual memories, satisfaction/return state and lapse history;
- never save every generated guest indefinitely.

### 11. Seasons and trends

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

Seasons and trends are automatic simulation systems, not manually curated live content.

Canonical decision:

- seasons follow the shared canonical calendar;
- effects remain bounded and understandable;
- trends spread gradually and plausibly;
- reach may be local/regional/global depending on the trend;
- yearly trend patterns must not repeat identically every in-game year;
- generation/spread must remain deterministic from game state/seed/time where needed for saves/fair comparison;
- trends never arbitrarily make a sound venue non-viable;
- the player observes and reacts but does not maintain the trend system.

The inherited requirement that slow shifts are signalled before material impact remains valid.

### 12. Events

**Status: SUPERSEDED / NOT CORE SCOPE**

Do not build a general event system merely because later preparation documents contain an `Events` heading.

Current decision:

- no player-created venue events system;
- no broad world-event taxonomy is required now;
- seasons, trends, press/reviews, market movement and ordinary operational variation already provide changing conditions;
- a narrowly scoped future event feature may be reconsidered only if playtesting identifies a concrete gameplay need.

Older references such as marketing/opening-event copy are flavor/marketing examples, not approval of an event-management system.

### 13. Memberships / subscriptions

**Status: NOW LOCKED AT SYSTEM LEVEL**

Memberships are approved as a later system.

Core behavior:

- membership costs materially more than one single visit but can be cheaper than repeated individual admissions for frequent users;
- adoption depends on expected use and fit, including satisfaction, repeat behavior, local convenience/reach, opening-hour fit, price/value and capacity experience;
- regulars are natural membership candidates;
- membership does not create demand independently of venue quality/fit;
- overcrowding can reduce satisfaction/retention and increase churn;
- exact products, prices and churn curves remain central balance data.

### 14. Shop and merchandise scope

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

The inherited small-shop model remains correct.

- a venue normally offers a small active assortment rather than a huge retail catalogue;
- stock replenishment remains automatic;
- merchandise/wellness product breadth should stay compact;
- broader product catalogue target is roughly **maximum 10 meaningful product types**;
- an individual venue may still offer only a smaller subset, consistent with the inherited roughly 3-5 active-product approach;
- branded merchandise requires genuine venue/program identity.

### 15. Site offers and affordability

**Old wording:** three generated financially viable offers; initial offers affordable.

**Status: CLARIFIED**

Canonical decision:

- every generated site must be strategically viable under at least one sensible path; no objective trap offers;
- affordability and attractiveness are separate dimensions;
- at game start, the initial offer set remains realistically accessible from starting cash/responsible borrowing as already locked;
- later in the game, not every offer must be immediately affordable;
- however, each fresh offer batch must contain **at least one financially realistic path for the player's current stage**;
- more expensive/unaffordable options may still appear as aspirational alternatives.

This means `viable` must not be interpreted as `immediately affordable right now` for every later offer.

### 16. Site attractiveness

**Status: NOW EXPLICITLY DEFINED**

Do not create one exposed universal `attractiveness score`.

A site's attractiveness is relative to price, strategy and concept. Important underlying dimensions include:

- acquisition/rent pressure and essential opening investment;
- reachable demand potential;
- hidden market mix and concept fit;
- price sensitivity/premium acceptance;
- daypart/weekday rhythm;
- usefulness of the starter base;
- expansion capacity/anchors;
- distinctive physical advantages such as natural water/outdoor potential;
- staffing/utilities/maintenance burden;
- destination/brand potential;
- seasonal exposure;
- concept flexibility.

The player sees truthful concrete clues, not raw hidden scores.

### 17. Selling a venue

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

Sale value continues to react to property/site value where applicable, installed investment, condition/wear, demonstrated earnings, local reputation/brand contribution and relevant debt/obligations.

New clarification:

- staff do not automatically transfer with the venue;
- reusable company knowledge and player-owned program IP remain with the chain unless a later explicit mechanic says otherwise.

### 18. Ranking and future scoreboard

**Status: CLARIFIED / FORMULA DEFERRED**

- ranking remains separate from local demand;
- fixed game time supports fair shared comparison periods;
- do not rank only by current cash;
- future Empire Score may combine business value, quality/reputation, successful venue operation and brand/portfolio performance;
- exact formula remains deliberately deferred because it changes player incentives;
- ranking never causes simulated rivals to steal/undercut local guests.

### 19. Content Studio

**Status: NEW LOCKED PRODUCTION REQUIREMENT**

The native/Xcode workspace should include a macOS **Sauna Empire Content Studio** for authoring and validating locations, start buildings, add-on modules, upgrades, anchors, routes and metadata.

It must clearly distinguish:

- Location
- Start Building
- Base-building Upgrade
- Add-on Module
- Module Upgrade
- Location-specific Module
- Shared Compatible Content

AI may propose content/layout fixes, but suggestions are visibly proposals and require approval.

Ordinary content authoring must not require editing Swift source.

See `CONTENT_STUDIO.md`.

### 20. Placeholder assets

**Status: CLARIFIED / APPROVED**

Final assets are not a prerequisite for implementation.

- placeholders may represent buildings/modules/characters/effects while systems are built;
- placeholder and final art must share the same authored footprint/anchor/data contract;
- replacing placeholder art must not change simulation IDs or gameplay behavior;
- AI-generated art remains draft until technical/visual approval.

### 21. Modular location/building relationships

**Status: CLARIFIED / STILL LOCKED**

The inherited fixed-composition/no-free-placement philosophy remains correct.

The production data model must explicitly represent:

- what a location can start with;
- which start buildings are legal there;
- which additions are purchased later;
- which upgrades belong to the start building;
- which upgrades belong to an add-on module;
- which fields/modules belong to the location itself;
- which shared modules are compatible;
- exact anchors/routes/occlusion/interaction metadata.

The player chooses **what** to build, while authored data controls **where/how** it can legally attach.

## Decisions that remain inherited and unchanged

Unless specifically reconciled above, all locked decisions in `docs/decision-log.md` remain in force on `ai-xcode-preparation`.

This includes, among other things:

- management-sim product identity;
- Aufguss terminology and system depth;
- generated site-offer expansion rather than free city selection;
- no objective trap sites;
- location + permanent base + compatible modular upgrades;
- player-set opening hours;
- automatic operations while offline;
- no pay-to-skip/mobile wait-gate design;
- visible guest activity with bounded character simulation;
- exterior-only primary venue scene;
- authored route graphs and fixed upgrade placement;
- top-down pixel-art visual direction;
- hidden market values communicated through qualitative evidence;
- regulars/retention as meaningful reputation inputs;
- simple loans and automatic repayment;
- no taxes/stocks outside the sauna business;
- ability to sell venues;
- local experience dominates local demand;
- Brand Value has capped effects;
- rank has no direct local economic advantage;
- no active rival system that steals local guests;
- data-card requirements for purchasable assets;
- deterministic online/offline simulation;
- year-round natural-water routes where the authored site supports them;
- the current approved Aufguss composition/execution/Venue Fit framework;
- all explicit approved asset/location/building ownership rules not changed above.

## Working assumptions that must not silently become product decisions

The `Working Assumptions For v0.1` section in the old decision log remains implementation guidance only unless separately approved.

In particular:

- UI-first prototype choices do not override the native SwiftUI + SpriteKit architecture;
- browser-local save assumptions do not define the final native save/cloud model;
- prototype implementation convenience does not override the canonical persistent time model;
- old browser framework choices do not constrain the final native runtime.

- exact hourly wage values and labor-cost balance;
- exact algorithm that automatically fits recurring Aufguss sessions into opening hours while preventing Master overlap;
- exact broker-search prices/cadence and later-game offer refresh balance;
- exact site/economy numeric values;
- exact trend frequency, spread curves and weights;
- exact membership price/churn parameters;
- exact merchandise catalogue contents within the approved small scope;
- final sale valuation weights;
- final Empire Score/ranking formula;
- numerical cross-venue balance;
- final asset production inventory and visual approvals.

These may be designed/tested without reopening already locked structural decisions.
