# Sauna Sim: Site Library v0.2

Last updated: August 23, 2026

## Reality Check

The library should resemble the types of buildings that are genuinely plausible for public sauna and wellness conversion. The strongest real-world patterns are:

- former warehouses, factories and light-industrial units, which offer open volume, robust services and an identifiable exterior
- railway warehouses, parcel rooms, yards and workshops, which already have an industrial public-facing character
- harbour warehouses, boathouses and service buildings, which pair naturally with water access
- former public baths, pools, laundries and bathhouse cellars, which already have a relationship to water, changing and public use
- rural mills, barns and former service buildings, mainly as lower-density destination sites

Examples include a former railway-station warehouse converted into Tullin Sauna in Tampere, a former ice-cream factory converted into Sauna House Greenville, and a historic industrial cellar proposed as a bath house in Birmingham. See [Tullin Sauna](https://studiopuisto.fi/work/tullin-sauna/), [Sauna House Greenville](https://www.lilesconstruction.com/portfolio/sauna-house-greenville), and [Birmingham Bath House](https://mo-st.com/projects/birmingham-bath-house/).

## Purpose

This is the first catalogue of 20 generated site templates. A player never sees the full catalogue. The generator presents three affordable, viable offers whose descriptions, price, surroundings and scale create a real choice.

Each template is a content identity, not a mechanically fixed outcome. The hidden market, price, condition and exact surroundings vary per generated offer.

## Production Rule

The library uses a small set of visual families. Twenty templates must not mean twenty separate art pipelines.

## Retired City Candidates

Dense city venue concepts are retired from active planning. The game retains urban demand through Canal, Harbour and Industrial District, but does not place player venues tightly between unowned buildings because it prevents convincing exterior growth. Do not implement or generate templates 5-9 from this older library.

## 1. Brick Waterfront

### 1. Canal Workshop

**Form:** Narrow former repair workshop beside a city canal.

**Scene:** Brick facade, a short canal edge, warm streetlights, cyclists and evening foot traffic.

**Promise:** Compact city ritual with direct water access.

**Pressure:** High recurring cost and tight internal capacity.

**Signature growth:** Canal deck, bathing bridge, lit entrance, compact roof plunge.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

### 2. Customs House Annex

**Form:** Small annex of an old harbour customs building.

**Scene:** Heavy brick, cobbled quay, mooring posts and active harbour movement.

**Promise:** A more serious, heritage-feeling waterfront sauna.

**Pressure:** Purchase or rent is expensive; preservation constraints make major changes costly.

**Signature growth:** Broad bathing steps, lantern-lit quay terrace, water-facing glass room.

**Availability:** Early expansion.

### 3. Inner Harbour Warehouse

**Form:** Former small harbour warehouse with a deep brick loading facade near the inner harbour.

**Scene:** Old loading doors, a narrow service street and water glimpsed beyond surrounding buildings.

**Promise:** Good potential for a visible reception, shop and a recognisable neighbourhood brand.

**Pressure:** Water access is indirect, so a true bathing bridge is unlikely; exterior space is scarce.

**Signature growth:** Program window, street terrace, roof plunge, evening signage.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

## 2. Urban Industrial

### 4. Northside Depot

**Form:** Low former freight depot in a mixed city district.

**Scene:** Broad facade, paved forecourt, bicycles, light road traffic and room behind the building.

**Promise:** Flexible capacity and easy operational growth.

**Pressure:** No natural water and less immediate destination character.

**Signature growth:** Planted forecourt, side wing, outdoor terrace, roof plunge.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

## 4. Timber Nature

### 10. Pine Lake Pavilion

**Form:** Existing timber pavilion at a forest lake.

**Scene:** Reeds, water, trees, small deck and a quiet access path.

**Promise:** Natural-water ritual and destination appeal.

**Pressure:** Low casual walk-in demand; reputation and concept must bring guests.

**Signature growth:** Bathing bridge, lakeside deck, fire area, outdoor sauna.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

### 11. River Mill House

**Form:** Restored small mill house beside a slow river.

**Scene:** Water wheel remnants, stone wall, riverbank grass and a small bridge.

**Promise:** Strong identity and a memorable program setting.

**Pressure:** Odd building layout; expansion is possible but must respect the site.

**Signature growth:** River steps, mill-yard terrace, small timber wing.

**Availability:** Early expansion.

### 12. Quarry Shelter

**Form:** Former quarry office by a flooded stone quarry.

**Scene:** Rock walls, dark water, sparse pines and a dramatic access path.

**Promise:** High-end destination atmosphere and unusually strong cold-water appeal.

**Pressure:** Access and construction are expensive; demand must justify the investment.

**Signature growth:** Stone deck, quarry plunge steps, glass lookout sauna.

**Availability:** Mid game.

### 13. Forest Service Building

**Form:** Compact former forest-service building at the edge of managed forest.

**Scene:** Tall trees, gravel path, timber equipment shed and a modest clearing.

**Promise:** Low-cost, intimate traditional sauna with space to grow.

**Pressure:** No direct water; a concept needs to create enough reason to travel.

**Signature growth:** Outdoor barrel plunge, fire circle, timber sauna cabin.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

## 5. Coast and Harbour

### 14. Marina Boathouse

**Form:** Long, low boathouse at a small marina.

**Scene:** Masts, pontoons, boats, wind and a broad wooden edge.

**Promise:** Strong water access and a natural social summer identity.

**Pressure:** Higher property value and more competition for the waterfront setting.

**Signature growth:** Wide bathing bridge, deck bar/service hatch, glass windbreak.

**Availability:** Early expansion.

### 15. Coastal Lifeboat Shed

**Form:** Robust former rescue shed near a rocky coast.

**Scene:** Salt grass, dark rocks, open water and a simple road approach.

**Promise:** A dramatic destination experience with an obvious exterior story.

**Pressure:** Remote setting; marketing, reputation and transport appeal matter.

**Signature growth:** Ocean steps, wind-protected deck, cliffside glass extension.

**Availability:** Mid game.

### 16. Ferry Terminal Storehouse

**Form:** Small storehouse at a local ferry landing.

**Scene:** Ferry movement, waiting pedestrians, quay markings and water traffic.

**Promise:** High visible flow and a memorable transit-adjacent venue.

**Pressure:** Busy flow can be shallow; the sauna must convert passers-by into real guests.

**Signature growth:** Quay sign, compact bridge, heated waiting deck.

**Availability:** Early expansion.

## 6. Rural Conversion

### 17. Orchard Packing Barn

**Form:** Small converted packing barn beside an orchard.

**Scene:** Fruit trees, gravel drive, open field and an older timber facade.

**Promise:** Soft rural identity with ample future space.

**Pressure:** Limited walk-in traffic and no natural water.

**Signature growth:** Orchard deck, outdoor plunge, glass greenhouse lounge.

**Availability:** Early expansion.

### 18. Garden Centre Service Building

**Form:** Former garden-centre service building with an attached greenhouse.

**Scene:** Glass structure, planting beds, warm light and a small roadside entrance.

**Promise:** Visually distinctive wellness concept and strong cosmetic potential.

**Pressure:** Heat and utility upgrades are costly; the concept needs to feel intentional, not decorative only.

**Signature growth:** Heated glass lounge, planted deck, water garden plunge.

**Availability:** Mid game.

### 19. Community Pool Annex

**Form:** Underused annex beside a modest community swimming pool.

**Scene:** Tiled entrance, pool building, local families and clear municipal surroundings.

**Promise:** Existing water-service logic and broad, accessible demand.

**Pressure:** Less exclusive first impression; premium positioning needs stronger service and programs.

**Signature growth:** Sauna entrance wing, program sign, premium recovery deck.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

## 7. Heritage Bathing

### 20. Grand Municipal Bathhouse

**Form:** A closed historic municipal bathhouse with a large existing service wing.

**Scene:** Formal historic facade, tiled entry, chimney, town-square presence and a walled rear court.

**Promise:** High-prestige wellness destination with an authentic water-and-bathing story.

**Pressure:** Significant purchase cost and careful, expensive renovation.

**Signature growth:** Restored entrance, grand courtyard pool, glass sauna wing, historic bath signage.

**Availability:** Late game.

## 8. Modular Starter Sites

### 21. Container Sauna Yard

**Form:** A leased or purchased hard-surface yard where the player installs converted shipping containers as the first sauna building.

**Scene:** Fenced urban or harbour-side yard, stacked or joined containers, simple lights, a small service gate and the possibility of a deck or plunge tank.

**Promise:** A visibly modular starter concept that begins with existing converted containers.

**Pressure:** Limited initial capacity and comfort; an exposed industrial yard needs investment before it supports a premium experience.

**Signature growth:** A second sauna container, joined changing container, deck, plunge tank, program sign and planted perimeter.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.

### 22. Existing Tent Camp

**Form:** A leased or owned plot with an existing robust temporary tent sauna and essential service setup.

**Scene:** Open grass, gravel or water-edge plot, tent sauna, simple changing tent, fire/heat point, portable lighting and a small entry sign.

**Promise:** The cheapest, quickest way to test a location and prove that a concept can attract people there.

**Pressure:** Low capacity, limited service spend and weak premium appeal until permanent construction begins.

**Signature growth:** Larger tent, deck, portable plunge and further tent-compatible modules; it remains a tent camp.

**Availability:** Starter or early expansion.
