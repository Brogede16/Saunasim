# Sauna Sim Ultimate Aufguss Patterns v0.1

## Purpose

`Ultimate` is the highest fixed Composition Tier. It is not a random roll, a purchase reward or an item rarity. A program becomes Ultimate only when its saved composition exactly matches one of these six authored patterns and has completed its first real session.

Ultimate patterns are intentionally rare discoveries. They are still normal programs operationally: the player must afford their materials, have a suitable Master, use a venue that can carry the promise and price the session honestly. A poor first delivery can therefore reveal an Ultimate composition with a low Execution Tier and a low overall star result.

## Rules

- `Ultimate` sits above `Iconic` for **Composition Tier only**. Execution Tier and Venue Fit remain capped at `Iconic`.
- It uses a compact platinum-white mark with a restrained warm shimmer in menus. It adds no bespoke world effect, character animation or automatic revenue multiplier.
- An Ultimate composition may contribute to a five-star result only when Execution Tier and Venue Fit are both `Iconic`. It never creates a sixth star.
- Any core composition edit returns the program to `Unproven` and removes Ultimate status until the revised program runs once.
- The suggested program name may be renamed freely. The name does not affect the pattern.
- A pattern requires the listed components exactly. The assigned Master, venue, price, session time, schedule and other unlisted facilities do not change the Composition Tier, although they strongly affect delivery and stars.
- The player is never shown this recipe table. Each pattern has at least two natural-language clues across existing cards, Steam Guide text or later Master/venue information.

## Balance Requirements

- The six patterns use different core promises: two Classic Rituals, two Quiet Recoveries, one Social Energy and one Show Journey. This gives each player style a discovery route without pretending all styles have identical operational needs.
- The three `Long` Ultimate patterns use three ordered aroma rounds because their value is a genuine sensory progression. The three `Standard` patterns use two rounds so a complete program does not automatically become long, expensive or difficult to schedule.
- No Ultimate is tied to a single location or building. `Venue Fit` makes some places much better homes for particular patterns, but every pattern remains compositionally discoverable anywhere it can physically run.
- There is no Ultimate material and no Ultimate-only item. Existing low-cost materials appear in two patterns; premium and event materials provide colour, not an automatic top tier.
- The balance pass must test that a discovered Ultimate with Normal execution/fit is not more profitable than a well-delivered Rare or Iconic standard program merely because of its mark.
