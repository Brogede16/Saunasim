# Sauna Sim Aufguss Starter Program Library v0.1

## Purpose

These are editable English starter presets, not prescribed recipes or a research tree. They give a player readable first examples of each intent and venue direction while leaving every material, music direction and free composition available from the start.

All presets begin `Unproven` when first run. Their Composition Tier is discovered from the actual component combination. The library never promises that a name, cost class or location will guarantee a tier.

## Availability and Readiness

- Every preset is visible in the Program Library from the start.
- A preset with missing facilities is visible but greyed out at the required recovery/program field. The card names the missing physical requirement.
- A player may remove the unsupported finish and run an edited core version immediately. Editing creates an `Unproven` variant.
- A preset may be run by any Master, but its card highlights relevant craft/style pressure rather than locking it to one person.
- The listed setting is a strong narrative/physical fit, not a restriction. A good player can adapt a preset elsewhere, with Venue Fit and execution revealing the consequence.

## Program Card Layout

Each preset card shows only player-useful facts:

```text
Birch After Work
Classic Ritual · Standard
Nordic Birch · Water Pour · Acoustic & Folk

Reliable warmth for a clear evening reset.

Works naturally with: Classic Towelwork, a Traditional Master.
Good to know: Its simple shape leaves room for the place around it.
```

The editor separately shows rounded room time, guest-journey time, material input cost, required physical finish and the player's selected price/frequency. It does not show a predicted tier.
