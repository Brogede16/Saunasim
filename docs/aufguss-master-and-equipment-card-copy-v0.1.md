# Sauna Sim Aufguss Master and Equipment Card Copy v0.1

## Purpose

This is the English player-facing copy for Master styles, craft ratings and the four approved equipment categories. It explains why a Master or tool helps a specific part of a Gus without making people, equipment or a high price into universal answers.

## Master Styles

### Traditional

**About**  
This Master is at home in clear, craft-led sauna work: controlled towel movements, recognisable rounds and a confident use of established ritual.

**Good to know**  
Traditional Masters naturally support Classic Towelwork, Classic Ritual, Acoustic & Folk, Silence & Natural Sound and wood or herb-led programs. They can run a show, but a highly choreographed concept needs enough Performance Craft to feel deliberate rather than merely formal.

### Meditative

**About**  
This Master gives the room space. Their strongest sessions use measured heat, clean transitions and a calm presence rather than constant activity.

**Good to know**  
Meditative Masters naturally support Quiet Ritual, Gentle Build, Ambient, Green Tea, Sage, Lavender and a Rest Deck finish. They can deliver bold heat, but a loud, fast program needs a clear structure so the energy does not work against their pace.

### Energetic

**About**  
This Master works visibly with pulse, movement and shared momentum. Their delivery can make a social room feel connected without turning every program into a party.

**Good to know**  
Energetic Masters naturally support Rhythmic Flow, Rhythmic Pulses, Pop & Disco, Electronic & Techno and a controlled High-Heat Finale. They can run a quiet ritual, but should slow their movement and let the material rather than the performance lead.

### Theatrical

**About**  
This Master is strongest when a Gus needs visible transitions and a memorable arc. They turn a well-built idea into an occasion rather than adding spectacle at random.

**Good to know**  
Theatrical Masters naturally support Story-led Performance, Choreographed Show, Film Scores, Ritual Percussion and distinctive Event Essences. They can deliver a simple traditional session, but the program should give their Performance Craft a real purpose instead of making the room feel over-produced.

## Craft Ratings

### Heat Craft

**About**  
Heat Craft measures how reliably a Master distributes heat and controls demanding changes in the room.

**Good to know**  
It matters most for Rhythmic Pulses, High-Heat Finale and fan-assisted delivery. It does not make weak scent choices coherent or replace a proper recovery route.

### Aroma Craft

**About**  
Aroma Craft measures how cleanly a Master handles dosage, ordered rounds and more demanding material forms.

**Good to know**  
It matters most for multiple aroma rounds, Herbal Infusion, Scented Ice Round, Botanical Water and Fresh Vihta. It does not turn an unrelated blend into a strong program by itself.

### Performance Craft

**About**  
Performance Craft measures control of pacing, movement and the room's shared attention.

**Good to know**  
It matters most for Rhythmic Flow, Choreographed Show, Story-led Performance and music-led transitions. It cannot make an overcrowded room or an unsupported venue fit disappear.

## Equipment Categories

### Towel Set

**About**  
A better towel set gives the Master a more reliable grip, weight and movement during visible towelwork.

**Good to know**  
Use it to improve Classic Towelwork and performance-led delivery. It helps the Master carry a planned rhythm, but it does not add heat capacity or make a basic program automatically premium.

### Hand Fan

**About**  
A hand fan gives the Master more precise control over where and when heat moves through the room.

**Good to know**  
Use it with demanding heat, Rhythmic Pulses or a High-Heat Finale. It supports Heat Craft; it does not make a finale comfortable when the room is crowded or guests have nowhere to recover.

### Infusion Kit

**About**  
An infusion kit keeps materials, ice and herb-led rounds organised and easier to deliver consistently.

**Good to know**  
Use it with Scented Ice Round, Herbal Infusion, Botanical Water or Fresh Vihta. It supports Aroma Craft; it does not lower material costs or remove the need for a clear aroma sequence.

### Rain Ladle

**About**  
A rain ladle creates a broad, even pour that can change the whole room in one controlled phase.

**Good to know**  
Use it for a deliberate stronger moment in a program that has enough time and Heat Craft to control the result. It is required for Rain Pour, and it should not be used to make every round equally intense.

## Copy Review Rules

- Master style is a delivery preference, never an identity label or a programme lock.
- Craft and tool descriptions must name their narrow practical contribution and at least one thing they do not solve.
- Individual item variants may add copy later, but they must inherit the limits of their equipment category.
