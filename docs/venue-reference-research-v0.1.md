# Sauna Sim Venue Reference Research v0.1

## Purpose

This note grounds the building-specific upgrade pass in real sauna and wellness offers. It is not a catalogue to copy. Sauna Sim should translate the strongest patterns into a manageable exterior-only game: a few visible, coherent interventions per venue rather than a giant generic spa menu.

## What Successful Venues Actually Sell

### 1. A complete heat-to-cold route

The strong offer is a sequence, not a lone sauna: heat, a credible cool-down/shower, water or cold plunge, then sheltered rest. Löyly explicitly combines sauna, private terrace and sea access; its winter offer includes ice swimming. The Well markets outdoor showers after its Forest Sauna, cold plunge, warm pools and outdoor relaxation as one experience. [Löyly private sauna](https://www.loylyhelsinki.fi/en/private-sauna), [The Well pools and showers](https://thewell.no/en/spa/pools-showers/)

**Game implication:** cold access, shower, wind shelter and recovery seating should be designed as connected routes. A water asset without approach, entry and return animation is incomplete.

### 2. View, shelter and outdoor architecture

High-end coastal venues make the outside usable rather than merely scenic. Löyly uses a timber outer layer for privacy, wind protection, shaded glass and several small cooling terraces; it also forms roof/view terraces and an outdoor auditorium. [Löyly story](https://www.loylyhelsinki.fi/en/the-loyly-story)

**Game implication:** where there is a good view, the compelling addition is typically a protected viewing/recovery place, steps, a terrace or a roof route. A raw panoramic window alone is weaker.

### 3. Group ritual and programmed differentiation

Large and premium places sell named rituals, time slots and different intensities, not merely hotter rooms. The Well distinguishes peaceful, hot/cold and social programme days, and prices some specialised rituals separately. [The Well weekly program](https://thewell.no/en/spa/your-spa-visit/weekly-sauna-program/)

**Game implication:** a real Aufguss space should be an identifiable room or outdoor stage with a route, guests and programme capability. It works especially well for social urban and industrial venues, while quiet nature venues can prioritise smaller ritual routes.

### 4. Place-specific water, not generic water

Copenhagen's floating saunas sit at designated harbour bathing zones and use a small sauna plus a defined dip zone; this is closer to the game's intimate Water Plot than to a broad floating resort. [City of Copenhagen, floating sauna at Fisketorvet](https://svoemkbh.kk.dk/svoemmeanlaeg/badesteder-i-havnen/flydende-saunaer/flydende-sauna-ved-fisketorvet)

Open sea puts real limits on floating structures: Löyly's early floating option was rejected because of waves and ice pressure. [Löyly story](https://www.loylyhelsinki.fi/en/the-loyly-story)

**Game implication:** floating sauna belongs only to sheltered Water Plot. Canal/harbour/coast must each have a different fixed shore/quay/sea-access solution. No universally available raft or bathing bridge.

### 5. The premium retreat sells calm, light and a larger bathing world

Skodsborg presents a historic badesanatorium turned modern luxury spa, combining light-filled spa, forest/water surroundings, food and a broad wellness offer. The Well sells scale through multiple bathing experiences, pools, rituals and relaxed outdoor areas. [Skodsborg history](https://skodsborg.dk/en/hotel-history/), [The Well spa](https://thewell.no/en/spa/)

**Game implication:** Country Estate and Kursted should get fewer but more architectural, expensive additions: sun terrace, winter garden/orangery, warm-water facility, outlook and an iconic program hall. They should not receive a dozen small generic props.

### 6. Adaptive reuse succeeds when the original building stays visible

Paimio's redevelopment work treats the historic sanatorium as a coherent whole and reopens sun balconies while linking spa use to outdoors and forest. A boathouse retrofit can preserve its maritime form while adding sauna/bathing below. [ALA, Paimio Sanatorium](https://ala.fi/work/paimio-sanatorium/), [Guysborough Boathouse](https://www.mlsarchitects.ca/work/guysborough-boathouse)

**Game implication:** warehouse bays, workshop ports, fish-house cold rooms, boathouse slips and estate barns should be activated, not erased by unrelated extensions. Each upgrade must name its existing anchor.

## Audience and Venue Fit

| Venue family | Strong guest promise | Suitable additions | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tent camp | Affordable ritual and atmosphere | Fire circle, timber shower, compact warm bath, view deck | Resort-scale glass/spa wings |
| Container compound | Urban/social modular energy | Roof terrace, outdoor program deck, courtyard bath, visible shower route | Pretending it is a country retreat |
| Timber cabin | Quiet, intimate nature recovery | Stone plunge, view platform, small warm bath | Large show/event facilities |
| Pavilion | View, group ritual and sheltered outdoor life | Folding facade, protected terraces, broad water access, outdoor Aufguss stage | Heavy industrial appendages |
| Boathouse / floating sauna | Water-first ritual | Fixed quay/pier, water access, sheltered deck, intimate recovery | Generic inland garden features |
| Workshop / depot | Characterful urban conversion | Existing yard program space, industrial shower, small courtyard warmth | New undefined industrial yards or hall-scale growth |
| Warehouse / fish house | Event-scale industrial destination | Existing bay activation, large programme hall, roof/catwalk route, big recovery court | Inventing more warehouse volume |
| Estate / Kursted | Premium calm and destination wellbeing | Sun terraces, orangery, outdoor warm water, historic program hall | Cheap scattered props |
| Rooftop | City view and elevated ritual | wind screens, roof route, roof water facility, sky-facing program pavilion | Ground-level garden language |

## Design Test for Every Future Proposal

An addition may be shown to the player only if all answers are clear:

1. What existing building or location anchor does it attach to?
2. Why would a real operator at this exact venue build it?
3. Which guest motivation does it make stronger?
4. Does it create capacity, a paid programme, premium price tolerance, return visits, or only visual identity?
5. Which guest route and animation makes it visible?
6. Is it a shared mechanics family with a new visual variant, or a genuinely new mechanic worth producing?
