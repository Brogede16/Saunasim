# Sauna Sim Aufguss Material Library v0.1

## Purpose

This is the approved first 30-item material library for the Aufguss editor. Materials are purchased through real per-session use, never unlocked by an artificial progression gate. The exact cash figures belong to the economy balance pass; price classes establish the intended spread now.

The library is informed by actual professional sauna concentrates, Nordic birch ritual and ice-infusion practice. It uses sensory language and gameplay effects, not medical claims. [Professional concentrate examples](https://www.pinoshop.de/saunakonzentrat-eukalyptus-menthol-1l.html), [Nordic birch infusion](https://www.spitzner-professional.de/products/saunaaufguss-nordische-birke), [ice infusion](https://www.saunagut.de/produkte/eisaufguss-kugel/der-eisaufguss), [vihta/vasta tradition](https://www.visitfinland.com/en/articles/10-sauna-tips-for-beginners/).

## Price Classes

| Class | Intended economics | First-library count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Everyday` | Low per-session cost; supports reliable routine programming. | 4 |
| `Craft` | Noticeably higher ongoing cost; rewards purposeful program fit. | 12 |
| `Premium` | High per-session cost; used when price, concept and delivery can justify it. | 6 |
| `Event` | High-cost, unusual essence for a clearly framed special program; never an automatic quality boost. | 8 |

Price class is not a universal quality score. A poorly matched Premium material is wasted money; a well-delivered Everyday material can make an excellent program.

## Cross-Venue Balance Rule

Every material family must be capable of contributing to strong programs at every compatible location and building family. Woods and herbs are not forest-only; citrus is not city-only; premium botanicals are not water-only. Place changes the story, marketing and likely guest fit, not the legality or base quality of a material.

The economy balance pass must ensure that each venue can support at least one viable low-cost routine program, one viable craft-led program and, where its physical capacity permits, one viable premium program. A location's water, atmosphere or specialist facilities may create different strengths, but may not make its core Aufguss repertoire inherently inferior.

## Material Cards

Each material has a small original **ingredient mark** in the editor, such as a leaf, sprig, berry or wood grain. It is not a copied product logo or real supplier brand. Clicking it opens an English information card with:

- `About`: short factual sensory/origin note;
- `In this program`: cost class, selected delivery method and relevant program tags;
- `Visual cue`: its approved subtle steam/accent family;
- `Good to know`: a concise, non-medical explanation of why it may fit or clash with the current Gus.

All cards use the shared qualitative tag and hint language in `aufguss-program-language-v0.1.md`. Materials, delivery forms and Event Essences must hint at mood, pacing and combination role through ordinary words, never raw compatibility percentages.

`aufguss-material-card-copy-v0.1.md` contains the approved factual/tone reference for all 30 material cards and six delivery-form cards. It must be used as the starting point for later player-facing copy variants.

## Delivery Forms

An aroma round combines one selected material with one delivery form. The form changes pacing, cost and Master requirement; it is not another arbitrary scent.

| Delivery form | Cost/requirement | What it changes in the program | Visual/animation consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Water Pour` | Base cost; all Masters | Standard aroma/heat round. | Ladle/infusion pose and normal steam. |
| `Scented Ice Round` | Small additional session cost; Infusion Kit improves execution | Creates a distinct pause and release within a program. | Ice/bucket overlay, denser timed steam and cooler accent phase. |
| `Herbal Infusion` | Higher session cost; benefits strongly from Aroma Craft | Adds a craft-oriented natural treatment to an aroma round. | Shared herb/bucket overlay and green/earthy accent, not one animation per herb. |
| `Fresh Birch Vihta` | Premium ritual add-on; only where the program/venue can present it credibly | Adds a traditional tactile/aroma ritual moment; it is not a generic scent multiplier. | One shared fresh-bundle overlay and restrained birch-leaf cue. |

`Fresh Birch Vihta` is based on the Finnish `vihta`/`vasta` bundle practice. Its final guest-contact framing and safety presentation require content review before implementation; it must never be presented as unwanted contact or a universal guest preference.

## Visual Scope Rules

- The 16 ingredient marks are compact UI assets, not 16 full world sprites.
- Steam uses the shared effect sheets. Materials select a small accent palette and intensity phase; no material gets a wholly new smoke simulation.
- `Scented Ice Round`, `Herbal Infusion` and `Fresh Birch Vihta` require only shared prop overlays: ice/bucket, herb/bucket and fresh bundle. Distinctive Materials and Event Essences change only ingredient marks, card copy and shared steam-accent palette; they do not create new world props or body animations.
- Ingredient icons appear in the Gus editor, Master equipment/card details and Steam Guide program mentions. They do not float above every guest or clutter the scene.
