# Sauna Sim Venue Offer Seed Library v0.1

## Purpose

This is the first reviewable offer seed for every locked building base. A seed supplies a credible default market/economy direction and a balanced route structure; later generated offers vary condition, asking price, local clues and exact compatible fields without changing physical plausibility.

It is deliberately content data, not scene production. No seed authorises a background, building sprite, route map or final price.

## Progression Bands

| Band | Seeds | Design reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-offer eligible | Urban Tent, Industrial Container, Pine Lake Cabin, Canal Repair Workshop, Northside Depot, selected Marina Boathouse | Each has a viable modest route and a readable pressure. |
| Early expansion | Beach Pavilion, Orchard Estate, Dockside Fiskehus, selected Harbour Warehouse | Stronger identity or capacity with a higher cost/complexity expectation. |
| Later expansion | Quiet Water Plot, Hotel Skyline Roof, Coastal Kursted, major Warehouse variants | Their price, structural/flow complexity or scarcity should become meaningful after one stable venue. |

No first-offer set may consist solely of water/destination venues or solely of value/routine venues. The generator combines contrasting bands while preserving financial viability.

## Required Review Per Seed

Before a seed turns into a generated offer family, it needs:

1. One property-card strength/strength/pressure set from the approved clue pool.
2. One starting-capacity envelope and realistic upgrade costs from the cross-venue balance pass.
3. The exact location-owned and building-owned modules available on that plot variant.
4. One `Normal`, one `Rare`, and one potential `Iconic` Venue Fit test case.
5. A guest-route commitment for every starting and purchasable functional facility.

The seeds deliberately defer only final numeric and visual information; they do not defer concept, target market or trade-off.
