# Location Upgrade Review v0.1

## Purpose

This is the **approved** owner-review inventory for location-owned upgrades only. It is deliberately separate from building upgrades. A location owns terrain, shore, water, promenade, clearing, roof surface or bounded outdoor plot fields; a building owns its entrance, facade, shop, sauna volume, service rooms and attached wings.

The review order is:

1. Approve each location's credible outdoor/terrain/water base facility.
2. Approve its second- and third-level upgrades where useful.
3. Review every building base against the frozen location fields.

Shared facilities such as showers, cold plunges, terraces, warm spa and Outdoor Gus Sauna reuse their approved functional and animation contracts. A location supplies a believable variant and field; it does not invent a parallel mechanic.

`Outdoor Gus Sauna` always means an enclosed outdoor-sited sauna/pavilion. Historic wording such as "outdoor program field", "stage" or "platform" must be interpreted as the authored field for that enclosed facility, never as open-air Aufguss.

## Review Rules

- A genuine facility normally has Level 1 plus Level 2 and Level 3 upgrades. A selective Level 4 is allowed only when it creates a distinct credible route, capacity channel or guest use rather than another numeric increment. Shared facilities may reuse their core upgrades, but each location package also needs a small number of distinctive opportunities that cannot be reduced to a recolour of Canal.
- Lights, planting, paths, murals and similar site presentation additions are one-step improvements unless a later design demonstrates a real second decision. Do not manufacture upgrade trees for every lamp or planter.
- Every approved item needs a completed-world graphic. Guest-usable items also need authored route anchors and a guest action or triggered effect.
- Every purchased item also needs a bounded gameplay effect: capacity/flow, Gus/recovery capability, targeted attraction, credible price acceptance or return/retention. Small lighting, planting, path and view additions normally work through visible promise and a narrow attraction/price-acceptance effect; they are not decorative-only purchases or universal income multipliers.
- The first chosen location + building base determines material and silhouette. This review locks function, compatibility, field class, route/effect needs and workload, not final art variants.
- Before final art, all legal location/building/facility combinations enter the automated balance sweep defined in `cross-venue-balance-protocol-v0.1.md`.
