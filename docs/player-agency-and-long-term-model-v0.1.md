# Sauna Sim Player Agency and Long-Term Model v0.1

## Rule

Staff execute the player's plan; they do not play the game for them. A mature chain should remove repetitive administration while preserving the choices that define a sauna concept, investment strategy and guest experience.

## Never Automated

The player always controls:

- venue price and opening schedule;
- Aufguss programs, pricing and desired frequency;
- marketing direction and spend;
- hiring/firing key people and the strategic use of their time;
- loans, sale, purchase and expansion decisions;
- construction projects and priorities;
- each venue's concept and response to important feedback.

Managers may show concise recommendations, but cannot accept them or change a strategic setting.

## What Staff Automate

Staff carry out the selected plan:

- Masters run scheduled programs;
- Hosts support the selected service/flow approach;
- Technicians repair assigned facilities;
- Operations Managers improve eligible chain purchasing, real operating-resource efficiency and concrete campaign effectiveness.

No staff role automatically expands the chain, chooses a concept or turns a poor strategic decision into a good venue.

## Long-Term Decision Pressure

- A venue has a limited number of concurrent projects and scarce key people. The player cannot optimise every place at once.
- Successful venues reach natural decision points: add capacity, deepen quality/recovery, raise price, alter programming, target a different audience or use the cash elsewhere.
- Different venue types and concepts are valid. A coherent promise earns trust, but the game does not punish variety or force an identical chain.
- Large chains are managed through meaningful exceptions, projects and opportunities rather than micro-management of every venue every day.

## Slow Trends and Content Saturation

Guest taste is not static forever, but trends must be slow, legible and actionable:

- A trend lasts long enough for the player to observe it, decide and respond. It never creates an immediate emergency timer.
- Trends influence attention toward experience qualities, such as quieter ritual, show-oriented programs, recovery focus, winter cold-water ritual or social evening visits. They do not arbitrarily invalidate a well-run sauna.
- Repeatedly offering the same program content across many venues can gradually lose novelty and attention. This is not a punishment for a coherent chain: reliable standard programs remain useful, but distinctive variations and location-appropriate programs create new opportunity.
- Feedback and reports reveal an emerging shift before it is fully reflected in demand. The player may ignore a trend, adapt one venue, create a new program or use marketing to serve a durable niche.
- Trends never come from local rival sabotage. They are a broad guest-interest/current-culture layer and remain separate from rank.

## Long-Term Success

The game supports several valid objectives: stable profit, exceptional programs, premium destinations, a respected coherent chain and a high Standing rank. The player opens the game for a new decision, opportunity or pattern that needs ownership judgement, not to collect timed rewards.
