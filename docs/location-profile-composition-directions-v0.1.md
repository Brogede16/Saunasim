# Location Profile Composition Directions v0.1

These are the intended site compositions for every legal profile. They are the visual brief for the explicit coordinates still being added to the placement board. Asset size never changes; only compatible placement, routes and the relation between assets changes.

## Canal

- Repair Workshop: working canal-yard sequence, with sauna volumes stepping away from the workshop and recovery near the water route.
- Boathouse: long quay composition, with sauna volumes along the quay and recovery pulled down toward water access.

## Harbour

- Container Compound: enclosed cargo-yard cluster opening toward the quay, with extra containers stepping along its rear edge.
- Boathouse: narrow waterside chain with a long deck and recovery closest to the basin.
- Warehouse: converted hall dominates inland; outdoor facilities gather at a protected quay yard.
- Fiskehus: small working-house core, cold-room saunas along one side and nets/terrace recovery by the quay.

## Industrial

- Saunatelt Camp: loose temporary camp across the paved yard, leaving visible service lanes between tents.
- Container Compound: long compound along the road, with an open planted courtyard at the opposite end.
- Repair Workshop: brick workshop spine, with recovery and water facilities in a softened side yard.
- Small Depot: compact ramp-facing venue with one quiet recovery corner away from the road.
- Warehouse: hall-bay conversion with a broad covered-yard program zone.
- Fiskehus: reused cold-store cluster, visually separate from the larger warehouse footprint.

## Forest Lake

- Saunatelt Camp: small clearing camp, with recovery and fire at the forest edge and water route held open.
- Container Compound: restrained timber-clad compound set back from the lake, looking toward a long water approach.
- Small Timber Cabin: intimate cabin-and-veranda composition, with shower and recovery tucked among trees.
- Pavilion: broad lakeside viewpoint, with program sauna and deck aligned toward the lake.
- Country Estate + Barn: house and barn spread across the meadow; spa and recovery sit in the garden rather than the working yard.
- Kursted: formal lake retreat with bath wings facing a calm terrace and a separate ritual court.

## Coast

- Small Timber Cabin: cliff-side cabin with small sheltered recovery behind it and view deck toward the sea.
- Pavilion: long panoramic edge, with glazing, deck and program use facing the sea.
- Country Estate + Barn: sheltered estate court away from wind, with the view and water route as a distinct destination.
- Boathouse: low water-facing chain by the cove steps, with recovery on the leeward side.
- Kursted: grand coastal frontage, with spa court sheltered behind the main building.

## Beach

- Saunatelt Camp: spaced dune camp with clear boardwalk approach and a separate sun/recovery zone.
- Container Compound: compact dune-edge compound, with plant screens between containers and beach activity.
- Small Timber Cabin: small beach cabin with a longer boardwalk/deck connection to the water route.
- Pavilion: open beach pavilion, with the quiet spa set back from the active Gus side.

## Rural Plot

- Saunatelt Camp: meadow camp with loose tents and recovery around a fire-safe clearing.
- Container Compound: timber-softened compound held near the road, with garden facilities facing away from it.
- Small Timber Cabin: orchard-side cabin with a small private garden circuit.
- Country Estate + Barn: large working court, barn sauna chain, and separate garden/spa side.
- Kursted: formal rural retreat using a long arrival approach, bath court and garden recovery.

## Water Plot

- Floating Sauna: pier arrival, starter float close to shore, then program/recovery floats distributed along the water route rather than clustered on one platform.

## Urban Lot

- Saunatelt Camp: deliberate pop-up yard with tents spaced around the constrained centre and the service entry held close to the street.
- Container Compound: dense vertical-feeling yard, with containers forming a narrow court and recovery sheltered from neighbours.

## Hotel Rooftop

- Rooftop Sauna: lift-to-reception arrival, sauna volumes distributed along the roof edge, and exclusive recovery/view assets on the quieter skyline side.
