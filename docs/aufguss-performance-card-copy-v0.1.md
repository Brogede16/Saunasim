# Sauna Sim Aufguss Performance Card Copy v0.1

## Purpose

This is the first English player-facing card-copy library for the five approved performance styles. A style describes how the Master carries heat, transitions and audience attention. It is a creative choice, not a hard requirement: every Master can run every style, but the right craft, music and heat plan make the result easier to execute well.

## Classic Towelwork

**About**  
Clear towel movements, steady pacing and visible control of the heat. Classic Towelwork keeps the attention on the sauna rather than on a staged performance.

**Good to know**  
Use it with Steady Heat, Progressive Rounds, Silence & Natural Sound, Acoustic & Folk or a Traditional Master. It can carry a premium ritual, but do not expect it alone to sell a large Show Journey; that promise needs more visible development.

## Quiet Ritual

**About**  
Measured pours, calm pauses and a restrained presence from the Master. Quiet Ritual gives scent, room and recovery time to matter without rushing the session.

**Good to know**  
Use it with Gentle Build, woods, herbs, Ambient, Green Tea, vihta or a Rest Deck finish. Avoid combining it with fast Rhythmic Pulses and loud party music unless the program deliberately changes character partway through.

## Rhythmic Flow

**About**  
Repeated towel arcs and movement timed to a clear pulse. Rhythmic Flow turns heat distribution into something the guests can follow together.

**Good to know**  
Use it with Rhythmic Pulses, Electronic & Techno, Rock & Metal, Pop & Disco or an Energetic Master. Do not use it with music that has no pulse and a very slow heat plan; the Master will look busy while the program has nowhere to go.

## Choreographed Show

**About**  
Planned sequences, larger movements and clear moments for the room to notice. Choreographed Show makes the Master part of the experience rather than only the person delivering heat.

**Good to know**  
Use it for Social Energy or Show Journey with a capable Master, enough room, a readable music cue and a heat curve that builds toward a moment. Avoid using it in a cramped, low-capacity sauna or with an ordinary short routine; the performance will promise more than the setting can deliver.

## Story-led Performance

**About**  
A Gus with a recognisable beginning, development and ending. Story-led Performance connects heat, scent and music into one idea without needing dialogue or a literal plot.

**Good to know**  
Use it with Progressive Rounds, Film Scores, Ritual Percussion, ordered materials and a recovery finish that feels like a conclusion. Do not choose it for unrelated aroma rounds or a simple Short format; guests need enough time to understand the journey.

## Copy Review Rules

- Performance copy must describe observable delivery, not claim a guest personality or demographic fit.
- It may name a practical requirement such as room scale, rhythm, time or craft. It must not turn creative pairings into hard locks.
- The card should help the player understand why a program felt coherent or disjointed after it runs, without exposing the tier formula.
- Before implementation, create 2-4 approved paraphrases for each card while preserving this version as the tone reference.
