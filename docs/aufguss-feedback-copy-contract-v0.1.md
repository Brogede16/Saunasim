# Sauna Sim Aufguss Feedback Copy Contract v0.1

## Purpose

This is the reusable English copy contract for first-run reviews, guest comments and Steam Guide observations. It makes the three rating layers understandable in human language: composition, execution and Venue Fit. It must diagnose a useful next decision without exposing formulas or inventing a false certainty.

## First-Run Review Card

Every newly created or revised `Unproven` program receives one compact review after its first completed session:

```text
PROGRAM NAME
Composition: Rare
Execution: Normal
Venue Fit: Rare
Overall: 2 stars
Guest note: "The heat built with the music instead of fighting it."
Watch for: The small cold plunge was close to capacity.
```

The card always gives one short reviewed sentence. If a clear weakness exists, it may give one `Watch for` line. It does not show raw points, a recipe score, a precise probability or a list of every contributing factor.

## Composition Observations

Use these when the program's materials, heat, performance, music and finish are the main reason for the result.

### Strong coherence

- "The opening gave the later heat somewhere to go."
- "The final round felt earned rather than added for effect."
- "The music kept the same promise as the scent and movement."
- "Each round changed the program without losing its direction."
- "The unusual note had a clear role in the journey."

### Mixed or weak coherence

- "The program had several good ideas, but they did not quite become one session."
- "The music asked for more energy than the heat ever delivered."
- "The strongest round arrived before the program had built toward it."
- "The final scent felt separate from the rest of the Gus."
- "This short format did not leave the story enough time to develop."

## Execution Observations

Use these when delivery, Master craft, equipment, workload, price or scheduling determines the result. Never blame a person with vague language; name the practical condition.

### Strong execution

- "The Master kept every heat change clear and controlled."
- "The towelwork gave the room a pulse without rushing it."
- "The scent rounds landed cleanly and in the right order."
- "The performance made the program's transitions easy to follow."
- "Guests felt the price matched what was delivered."

### Mixed or weak execution

- "The finale needed steadier heat control to feel safe and satisfying."
- "The Master's movement was stronger than the program's quiet promise."
- "The aroma rounds needed more control to stay distinct."
- "The show had good moments, but the transitions lost their shape."
- "Guests liked the idea more than the value they received for the price."

## Venue Fit Observations

Use these when physical setting, building character, visible facility, flow or local market rhythm determines the result.

### Strong Venue Fit

- "The water finish made the whole program feel rooted in this place."
- "The room and recovery route gave the finale a proper landing."
- "The venue's scale made the shared rhythm feel intentional."
- "The visible setting promised exactly the experience guests received."
- "This programme gave local guests a strong reason to return."

### Mixed or weak Venue Fit

- "The program needed more recovery space after the final round."
- "The show outgrew the room available for it."
- "Guests could see the water, but the program had no route to use it."
- "The setting suggested a quiet escape while the program stayed too loud."
- "The schedule did not meet the local rhythm this program was trying to serve."

## Guest Comment Voice

Guest comments are short, personal and non-technical. They can refer to what the guest actually experienced, but must not make medical claims, insult a Master or make demographic assumptions.

### Positive examples

- "I came for the lake, but the birch rounds were what stayed with me."
- "It felt lively without being too much."
- "That cold finish was exactly what the final heat needed."
- "I would bring someone to this one."

### Constructive examples

- "I liked the music, but I could not tell what the session wanted to be."
- "The shower queue took the calm out of the ending."
- "The last round was good, just too sudden for me."
- "It looked like a special Gus, but it felt very short."

## Steam Guide Voice

Steam Guide is informed and concise, never snobbish. It comments on craft and value rather than pretending to have an objective universal taste.

### Examples

- "A compact ritual with an unusually clear heat progression."
- "Its quiet middle round makes the premium finish feel earned."
- "The idea is stronger than this first delivery; a more suitable Master could unlock it."
- "A fine social Gus that needs more recovery capacity before it can carry its current price."
- "The venue turns a familiar citrus program into a convincing destination session."

## Generation Guardrails

- Generate feedback only from reviewed sentence fragments tied to real recorded factors.
- Prefer one useful cause over a long list of minor influences.
- Do not repeat the exact same sentence for the same program on consecutive runs.
- A positive comment must not conceal a material capacity, price or delivery problem if that problem materially hurt the result.
- Neither guests nor Steam Guide reveal hidden tier thresholds, exact scores or Ultimate recipes.
